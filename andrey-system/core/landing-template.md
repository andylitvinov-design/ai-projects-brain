# Landing Template — Andrey Li System

This file defines a stable landing page structure for Andrey Li's offers, method, diagnostics, reports, and support programs.

Use this template to create clear, practical, conversion-focused landing pages.

Core principle:

client pain → hidden mechanism → resource → next level → concrete action/result

Landing pages must not overload the visitor with theory. They must make the offer clear, credible, and actionable.

---

## 1. Landing Goal

A landing page must answer quickly:

- Who is this for?
- What problem does it solve?
- Why is the problem happening?
- How does Andrey work with it?
- What result can the client expect?
- What should the client do next?

Primary conversion actions:

- request express diagnosis
- send a message
- book/support request
- request business analysis
- request extended report

---

## 2. Default Page Structure

Use this structure for most offers:

1. Hero
2. Problem
3. Hidden Mechanism
4. Method
5. Result
6. Offer / Product Format
7. How It Works
8. Who It Is For
9. Credibility / Proof
10. Safety / Boundaries
11. FAQ
12. Final CTA

For short landing pages, use:

1. Hero
2. Problem
3. Method
4. Result
5. Offer
6. CTA

## 3. Hero Block

### Goal

Make the visitor instantly understand the promise and relevance.

### Must Include

- who it is for
- main pain
- core result
- CTA

### Formula

If you feel stuck in [area], I help you identify the hidden mechanism, restore resource, and move toward [result].

### Example

If you feel stuck in energy, money, health, or direction, I help you see the hidden mechanism behind the block, restore resource, and return clarity, confidence, and movement.

### CTA Options

- Request express diagnosis
- Send your situation
- Get a resource analysis
- Request business analysis

---

## 4. Problem Block

### Goal

Show the client that Andrey understands their real state.

### Must Include

Use client language:

- low energy
- no clarity
- repeating problems
- money does not grow
- internal resistance
- health or psychosomatic pressure
- feeling stuck
- wanting more but not moving

### Structure

You may feel:

- you understand a lot, but still do not move
- energy disappears at the key moment
- money, business, or decisions are stuck
- the same pattern repeats
- the body or psyche signals pressure
- you need clarity, support, and direction

### Rule

Do not blame the client. Show that the problem has a mechanism.

---

## 5. Hidden Mechanism Block

### Goal

Differentiate Andrey from generic coaching, therapy, or advice.

### Core Message

The problem is not only the visible symptom. Behind it there is a mechanism:

Impulse → Compression → Defense → Repetition → Exhaustion

### Client-Friendly Explanation

At first there is an impulse: desire, energy, movement, or life force.
Then the system compresses: fear, trauma, stress, or protection blocks the movement.
A defense appears.
The same pattern repeats.
Eventually the client feels exhaustion, stagnation, symptoms, or loss of direction.

### Conversion Point

When the mechanism becomes visible, it becomes possible to restore resource and change the pattern.

## 6. Method Block

### Goal

Explain how Andrey works simply and credibly.

### Core Method

Andrey works with:

- hidden mechanism
- resource state
- Wu Xing analysis
- psychotherapeutic understanding
- resonant remedies/support tools when relevant
- practical actions

### Simple Version

I do not only give advice. I identify the mechanism that creates the block, show where resource is weak, and help restore the system so movement becomes possible.

### Method Structure

1. Diagnose the current state.
2. Identify the hidden mechanism.
3. Find the main resource bottleneck.
4. Reduce compression and repetition.
5. Strengthen resource.
6. Translate the new state into action.
7. Stabilize the result.

## 7. Resource / Wu Xing Block

### Goal

Make the method tangible without making it too theoretical.

### Core Message

Resource is not abstract. It can be seen through five practical dimensions:

- Water — energy, safety, deep reserve
- Wood — direction, action, growth
- Fire — expression, visibility, joy, contact
- Earth — stability, support, material base
- Metal — clarity, value, boundaries, structure

### Client-Friendly Message

When one of these areas is weak or blocked, life, business, money, health, or decisions can stop moving.

### Use Carefully

Do not make this block too long. It should create trust, not become a lecture.

---

## 8. Result Block

### Goal

Show what changes for the client.

### Results To Mention

Depending on the offer:

- clarity
- reduced inner tension
- more energy
- stronger confidence
- better decisions
- restored movement
- practical next steps
- business or money direction
- understanding of the block
- personal resource map

### Strong Result Formula

You receive not only an explanation, but a map:
where you are now → what blocks movement → what resource to restore → what next step to take.

### Avoid

- guaranteed healing
- guaranteed income
- vague miracle language

## 10. How It Works Block

### Goal

Reduce uncertainty and make the process safe.

### Structure

1. You send your request.
2. I analyze your current state and hidden mechanism.
3. I identify the resource bottleneck.
4. You receive diagnosis, recommendations, or a support plan.
5. If needed, we continue with monthly support or deeper work.

### Rule

Make the process feel simple and doable.

---

## 11. Who It Is For Block

### Goal

Help the visitor self-select.

### Good Fit

This is for you if:

- you feel stuck and want clarity
- your energy is low or unstable
- money or business does not grow
- you repeat the same pattern
- you want not just advice, but a mechanism map
- you are ready to take practical steps

### Not Ideal If

This may not be for you if:

- you want instant miracles
- you do not want to look at the mechanism
- you need emergency medical or psychological care
- you expect guaranteed medical or financial results

---

## 12. Credibility / Proof Block

### Goal

Create trust without exaggeration.

### Possible Elements

Use only true and provided facts:

- Andrey's experience
- method explanation
- examples of situations analyzed
- client stories if available and anonymized
- before/after state shifts if documented
- reports or diagnostic examples
- areas of expertise

### Rule

Do not invent proof, numbers, testimonials, medical results, or income claims.

---

## 13. Safety / Boundaries Block

### Goal

Make the offer responsible and credible.

### Include When Relevant

- This work does not replace medical care.
- No medical guarantees are made.
- The method supports clarity, resource, self-understanding, and practical movement.
- For serious medical or psychological emergencies, appropriate professional help is needed.

### Tone

Short, calm, professional.

---

## 14. FAQ Block

Use 4–7 questions.

Possible questions:

### What happens in express diagnosis?
You send your situation. I identify the main mechanism, resource bottleneck, and next step.

### Is this psychotherapy, coaching, or energy work?
The method combines psychotherapeutic understanding, resource diagnostics, Wu Xing, system thinking, and support tools. The focus is not labels, but finding the mechanism and restoring movement.

### Do I need to believe in Wu Xing or homeopathy?
No. The main value is a clear diagnosis of the mechanism, resource, and next step. Support tools are used when relevant.

### Can this help with business or money?
Yes, when money or business is blocked by resource, clarity, action, visibility, value, or structural bottlenecks.

### Do you guarantee results?
No guaranteed medical or financial outcomes. The work helps identify mechanisms, restore resource, and support practical movement.

### CTA Options

---

## 16. Landing Quality Check

Before finalizing a landing page, check:

- Is the target client clear?
- Is the pain concrete?
- Is the hidden mechanism explained simply?
- Is Andrey's method differentiated?
- Is the result clear?
- Is the offer easy to understand?
- Is there a clear CTA?
- Is there too much theory?
- Is there mystical overload?
- Are there unsupported claims?
- Is the landing connected to money, energy, clarity, movement, health support, or quality of life?

---

## 17. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- starting with abstract philosophy
- overexplaining Wu Xing before the visitor feels understood
- selling only sessions instead of movement
- vague phrases like deep transformation without concrete result
- guaranteed healing or income claims
- too many offers on one page without hierarchy
- CTA hidden at the bottom only
- long mystical language without client benefit
