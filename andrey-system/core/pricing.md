# Pricing — Andrey Li System

This file defines current prices, pricing hypotheses, pricing logic, and offer escalation rules for Andrey Li's Business Builder system.

Use this file for offers, landing pages, sales messages, client recommendations, and product packaging.

Important:
prices marked as "hypothesis" are not final. They should be tested and updated based on demand, conversion, client quality, and delivery effort.

---

## 1. Pricing Principle

Do not price only by session time.

Price by:

- clarity created
- resource restored
- depth of diagnosis
- level of support
- business value
- written deliverables
- implementation support
- transformation depth

Core sales logic:

Do not sell isolated sessions. Sell movement:
current state → clearer state → stronger resource → practical result.

## 7. Upgrade Logic

### From Express Diagnosis → Monthly Support

Use when:
- client pattern repeats
- one diagnosis is not enough
- client needs stabilization
- client asks "what now?"

Message:

The diagnosis shows the mechanism. Monthly Support helps stabilize the change and restore resource step by step.

### From Express Diagnosis → Extended Report

Use when:
- client wants a written map
- request is complex
- client needs documentation

Message:

If you want a deeper written map of your state, mechanism, resource, and next steps, an Extended Report is the best next step.

### From Business Resource Analysis → Monthly Support

Use when:
- business block is connected to personal resource/action/visibility
- client needs implementation support

Message:

The analysis identifies the bottleneck. Monthly Support helps you implement the next steps and stabilize the resource needed for business movement.

### From Monthly Support → Deep Transformation

Use when:
- deeper pattern keeps repeating
- client needs more intensive work
- issue is complex or long-standing

Message:

Monthly Support gives stabilization. Deep Transformation is for deeper mechanisms that need more focused work and integration.

## 8. Discount And Donation Rules

Use donation/free entry only for Express Diagnosis or introductory events.

Do not discount deep work too much. It lowers perceived value and may attract low-commitment clients.

Possible flexible options:
- donation entry
- limited beta price
- first report at test price
- package price for existing clients

Avoid:
- unclear discounts
- apologizing for price
- lowering price before value is explained

---

## 9. Pricing Quality Check

Before using a price in public or private sales, check:

- Is the result clear?
- Is the format clear?
- Is the price connected to value, not only time?
- Is the next step clear?
- Is it clear whether the price is fixed or experimental?
- Does the offer avoid medical or financial guarantees?
- Does the price support business sustainability?
