## 3. Example Mini Client Report

### Task

Create a mini-report for a client who has low energy, confusion, and cannot move forward in business.

### Output

# Mini Diagnostic Report

## 1. Current State

You describe a state where there is desire to move forward, but energy and clarity disappear when it is time to act.

Visible symptoms:
- low energy;
- confusion about direction;
- avoidance of action;
- business does not move as expected;
- inner tension before decisions.

Current level:
about 2.8–3.1 — compression with first attempts to stabilize.

## 2. Hidden Mechanism

The main mechanism looks like:

impulse → compression → protection → delay → exhaustion

There is an impulse toward growth and business movement. But when the system approaches visibility, decision, or responsibility, compression appears. The protective strategy slows action and creates delay. After that comes exhaustion and self-doubt.

Simple explanation:
You are not blocked because there is no potential. You are blocked because the system does not yet feel enough resource and safety for the next level of action.

## 3. Wu Xing Resource Analysis

Water — weakened base energy. The system needs deeper reserve and safety.

Wood — blocked action and direction. There is growth impulse, but it does not fully become movement.

Fire — reduced visibility and expression. There may be hesitation to show yourself or your offer.

Earth — needs more grounding and stable routine.

Metal — needs clearer structure, boundaries, and value formulation.

Main bottleneck:
Water + Wood. Energy and action must be restored first.

## 4. Target Level

Current level:
2.8–3.1 — compression and unstable movement.

Target level:
4.0 — more support, clarity, confidence, and ability to act.

Expected movement:
from inner pressure and delay → to clearer direction and small consistent actions.

## 5. Practical Actions

1. Reduce the next business step to one small action that can be done in 30 minutes.
2. Write one clear offer sentence: who you help, with what problem, and what result they receive.
3. Track energy before and after action to see where compression appears.
4. Do not force a large breakthrough yet. Stabilize repeated small movement first.

## 6. Recommended Next Step

Monthly support is recommended if the pattern repeats and you need tracking, correction, and stabilization.

Focus for the next session:
clarify the business bottleneck and strengthen the resource needed for action.

## 8. Style Rules Demonstrated By These Examples

Good outputs:
- start from real client pain;
- name the hidden mechanism;
- connect to resource;
- give a next level or next step;
- avoid vague promises;
- stay practical;
- do not overload with theory;
- sell movement, not isolated sessions.

Bad outputs:
- start with abstract philosophy;
- use too much mystical language;
- promise guaranteed healing or income;
- sell only a session without result;
- give advice without mechanism;
- produce long text without action.
