# Offer Library — Andrey Li System

This file defines reusable offers for Andrey Li's business system.

Use these offers as stable starting points for landing pages, sales messages, content, client recommendations, and product packaging.

Core principle:

Do not sell isolated sessions. Sell movement:
current state → clearer state → stronger resource → practical result.

---

## 1. Offer Map

Main offer ladder:

1. Express Diagnosis — entry point
2. Monthly Support — core product
3. Business Resource Analysis — business-focused diagnostic product
4. Deep Transformation — advanced personal work
5. Extended Client Report — standalone or add-on report product
6. Personal Resource Strategy — structured growth plan

Sales path:

content → express diagnosis → clear next step → monthly support / business analysis / deep transformation

---

## 2. Express Diagnosis

### Role

Entry product. Builds trust through precision.

### Target Client

People who feel stuck and want to understand what is happening before committing to deeper work.

### Client Pain

- I feel stuck.
- I have low energy.
- I do not understand what to do next.
- Money, business, health, or relationships do not move.
- I need clarity quickly.

### Hidden Mechanism

The client usually sees only the symptom, not the internal mechanism:
impulse → compression → defense → repetition → exhaustion.

### Desired Result

The client receives a first clear map:
- what is happening
- where the main block is
- what resource is weak
- what next step makes sense

### Format

- short written diagnosis
- voice diagnosis
- optional short call
- free or donation-based

### Deliverables

- current state summary
- main mechanism
- resource bottleneck
- current level
- recommended next step

### Core Promise

You will understand why you are stuck and what needs to be restored first.

### CTA

Send your request and I will make a short express diagnosis.

### Next Step

Monthly Support or Business Resource Analysis.

## 8. Offer Selection Rules

Use Express Diagnosis when:
- client is cold or uncertain
- trust must be built
- request is unclear
- client wants first clarity

Use Monthly Support when:
- client needs stabilization
- pattern repeats
- change requires tracking
- client is ready for ongoing work

Use Business Resource Analysis when:
- request is about money, clients, business, visibility, offers, sales, or decisions

Use Deep Transformation when:
- pattern is old, strong, complex, or psychosomatic
- client needs deeper work than one diagnosis

Use Extended Client Report when:
- client wants a written map
- value must be tangible
- work needs documentation

Use Personal Resource Strategy when:
- client wants growth and higher quality of life
- the focus is development, not only pain relief

## 10. Quality Check For Offers

Every offer must answer:

- Who is it for?
- What pain does it solve?
- What hidden mechanism does it reveal?
- What result does the client receive?
- What is the format?
- What are the deliverables?
- What is the next step?
- How does it lead to money, clarity, energy, movement, or growth?

Avoid:
- vague transformation
- only selling sessions
- only selling diagnosis
- medical guarantees
- mystical overload
- unclear CTA
