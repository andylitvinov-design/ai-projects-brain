# Client Intake — Andrey Li System

This file defines how to collect client information before express diagnosis, client reports, business analysis, monthly support, and deeper work.

Use this intake to make diagnostics clearer, safer, more practical, and easier to convert into the right offer.

Core principle:

collect only the information needed to understand:
client pain → hidden mechanism → resource → next level → concrete action/result

---

## 1. Purpose

Client intake helps identify:

- what the client wants
- where they feel stuck
- what visible symptoms appear
- what repeats
- where resource is weak
- what area of life/business is affected
- what result the client wants
- which offer fits best

Do not turn intake into a long questionnaire unless the task requires depth.

---

## 2. Privacy And Safety Rules

Do not collect unnecessary private data.

Do not store:
- secrets
- passwords
- tokens
- private documents
- highly sensitive medical records
- identifiable client stories without permission

Use anonymized summaries when creating reports or examples.

Medical boundary:
This work does not replace medical care. Do not make medical guarantees. If the client describes serious medical, psychiatric, or emergency symptoms, recommend appropriate professional help.

---

## 3. Short Intake — Express Diagnosis

Use this when the client is new or the goal is a fast first diagnosis.

Ask 5 questions:

1. What is your main request right now?
2. Where do you feel stuck: energy, money, business, health, relationships, direction, or something else?
3. What repeats again and again?
4. What do you feel when you try to move forward?
5. What result would be useful for you after this diagnosis?

Optional scales:

- Energy now: 0–10
- Clarity now: 0–10
- Confidence now: 0–10
- Ability to act now: 0–10

---

## 4. Standard Intake — Client Report / Monthly Support

Use this for reports, monthly support, or a deeper personal diagnostic.

### A. Request

- What is your main request?
- Why is this important now?
- What would be a good result for you?

### B. Current State

- How is your energy now, from 0 to 10?
- How clear is your direction now, from 0 to 10?
- How confident do you feel, from 0 to 10?
- How easy is it to act, from 0 to 10?
- What is the hardest moment during the day/week?

### C. Area Of Life

Which area is most affected?

- health / body
- money
- business / career
- relationships
- sexuality / love
- family
- self-realization
- life direction
- emotional stability

### D. Repeating Pattern

- What pattern repeats?
- When did you first notice it?
- What usually triggers it?
- What do you do to protect yourself when it appears?
- What happens after that?

### E. Body / Emotional Signals

- Where do you feel tension or pressure in the body?
- What emotion is strongest?
- What do you feel when you imagine taking the next step?
- What gives relief, even temporarily?

### F. Resource

- What gives you energy?
- What drains you most?
- What support do you already have?
- What support is missing?
- What helps you return to yourself?

### G. Previous Attempts

- What have you already tried?
- What helped?
- What did not help?
- What are you tired of repeating?

### H. Desired Next Level

- What would change if the problem improved?
- What level do you want to reach in the next 30 days?
- What practical action would become possible?

## 5. Business Intake — Business Resource Analysis

Use this when the request is about money, clients, sales, business, offers, visibility, or decisions.

### A. Business Context

- What do you do / sell?
- Who are your clients?
- What is your current main offer?
- What is your current price or price range?
- Where do clients come from now?

### B. Main Business Problem

- What is not moving: clients, money, sales, visibility, offer, action, confidence, strategy?
- What have you already tried?
- What result do you want?

### C. Money / Growth

- What monthly income do you have now, approximately?
- What monthly income do you want?
- What feels difficult about receiving more money?
- What happens internally when you think about raising prices or selling more?

### D. Action / Visibility

- What action are you avoiding?
- What happens when you need to show yourself or your offer?
- What do you fear people will think?
- What business step feels most blocked?

### E. Offer / Product

- What exactly do you offer now?
- What result does the client receive?
- Is the offer clear in one sentence?
- What part of the offer feels weak?

### F. Resource Bottleneck

Rate from 0 to 10:

- energy to work
- clarity of offer
- confidence in value
- ability to sell
- visibility readiness
- structure / organization
- consistency

### G. Desired Business Result

- What would be a realistic next step in business?
- What would make the next 30 days successful?
- What do you want to stabilize first?

Use when resource analysis is needed.

Ask the client to rate each from 0 to 10:

### Water — Energy / Base / Safety

- How much deep energy do you have?
- Do you feel safe enough to rest and recover?
- Do you feel inner reserve?

### Wood — Direction / Action / Growth

- Do you know where to go next?
- Can you take action?
- Do you feel growth impulse?

### Fire — Expression / Visibility / Joy

- Do you feel joy or inspiration?
- Can you connect with people?

## 7. Offer Routing From Intake

Use the answers to recommend the right offer.

### Express Diagnosis

Use when:
- request is unclear
- client is new
- client needs first clarity
- there is not enough data yet

### Extended Client Report

Use when:
- client wants a written map
- request is complex
- many areas are involved
- client needs structured explanation

### Monthly Support

Use when:
- pattern repeats
- client needs stabilization
- one diagnosis is not enough
- client needs tracking and correction

### Business Resource Analysis

Use when:
- request is about money, clients, sales, business, visibility, offers, or decisions

### Personal Resource Strategy

Use when:
- client wants growth and next level
- focus is development, not only pain relief

### Deep Transformation

Use when:
- pattern is strong, old, complex, or psychosomatic
- deeper work and integration are needed

## 9. RU Short Intake Message

Use this message for a new Russian-speaking client:

Чтобы сделать короткую диагностику, ответьте, пожалуйста, на 5 вопросов:

1. Какой у вас главный запрос сейчас?
2. Где вы чувствуете, что застряли: энергия, деньги, бизнес, здоровье, отношения, направление или другое?
3. Что повторяется снова и снова?
4. Что вы чувствуете, когда пытаетесь двигаться вперёд?
5. Какой результат был бы для вас полезен после диагностики?

Дополнительно можно оценить по шкале 0–10:
энергия, ясность, уверенность, способность действовать.

## 11. Intake Quality Check

Before using intake data, check:

- Is the request clear?
- Is the affected life/business area clear?
- Is the repeating pattern described?
- Is the current state measurable enough?
- Is there a desired result?
- Is the resource bottleneck visible?
- Is the next offer clear?
- Is any medical/emergency boundary needed?

If information is missing, ask only the minimum necessary follow-up questions.

---

## 12. Main Rule

Intake is not bureaucracy.

It is a diagnostic doorway.

The goal is to collect enough information to see the mechanism, identify resource, choose the next level, and recommend the right next step.
