# Product Structure — Andrey Li System

This file defines how the method becomes products and revenue.

## Product Principle

Do not sell sessions as isolated calls.

Sell movement:
current state → clearer state → stronger resource → practical result.

## Product Ladder

### 1. Entry Product — Express Diagnosis

Format:
- free or donation-based;
- short written or voice diagnosis;
- optional short call.

Purpose:
- show where the client is stuck;
- identify the main mechanism;
- name the resource bottleneck;
- create trust through precision;
- lead to monthly support.

Output:
- short diagnosis;
- current level;
- main block;
- next recommended step.

### 2. Core Product — Monthly Support

Format:
- 1 month;
- about 200 EUR/month;
- 2 sessions;
- diagnostics, resource work, remedy selection, and tracking changes.

Purpose:
- move client by 1 realistic level;
- reduce compression;
- strengthen resource;
- stabilize new behavior.

Core promise:
clarity, energy, confidence, and movement.

### 3. Advanced Product — Deep Transformation

Format:
- extended work for complex cases;
- deeper psychotherapeutic and resource analysis;
- more detailed report and support plan.

Purpose:
- work with deeper patterns;
- stabilize larger change;
- support long-term growth.

### 4. Business Analysis Product

For entrepreneurs and self-employed clients.

Focus:
- where business growth is stuck;
- what internal mechanism blocks money/action;
- what resource is weak;
- what direction and next move are most important.

Output:
- business/resource diagnosis;
- bottleneck;
- priority actions;
- support plan.

### 5. Report Product

Standalone or included in support.

Purpose:
- make the method tangible;
- create a reusable artifact;
- increase perceived value;
- support automation.

Use core/report-template.md.

## Upsells

- extended report;
- second diagnostic layer;
- business strategy session;
- monthly continuation;
- additional support tools;
- ritual / artifact when relevant and safe.

## Pricing Notes

Current known price:
- monthly support: about 200 EUR/month for 2 sessions.

Future pricing to test:
- express diagnosis: free / donation;
- written report: needs verification;
- business analysis: needs verification;
- premium transformation: needs verification.

## Sales Path

1. Client sees content.
2. Client requests express diagnosis.
3. Express diagnosis reveals mechanism and current resource level.
4. Client receives clear next step.
5. Offer monthly support or deeper product.

## Product Quality Rules

Every product must include:
- clear client problem;
- mechanism explanation;
- resource diagnosis;
- next level;
- practical actions;
- next step.

Avoid:
- vague consulting;
- only explaining without movement;
- only diagnostics without growth;
- too much theory before client feels understood.
