## Topic

Why you are stuck is not laziness.

## Post

Sometimes a person thinks:

"I am lazy. I just need more discipline."

But often the real problem is not laziness.

It is compression.

Inside there is an impulse to move, grow, earn more, become visible, or change life.

But the system does not have enough resource to hold this movement.

Then the psyche creates a defense:

- delay;
- doubt;
- avoidance;
- fatigue;
- loss of clarity.

From the outside it looks like laziness.

From the inside it feels like:

"I want, but I cannot move."

In my work, I first look for the hidden mechanism:

what exactly compresses the movement and where the resource is lost.

When the resource returns, action becomes easier.

Not forced.

Natural.
