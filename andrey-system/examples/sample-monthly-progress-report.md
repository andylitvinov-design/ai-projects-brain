## 2. Previous State

Previous Dao level: 3.0–3.5.

## 3. Current State

Current Dao level: 3.5–4.0.

Current stage: King / Queen — first manifestation.

---

## 4. What Improved

Resource: more stable energy during the week.

Behavior: less postponement, more ability to complete small actions.

Emotional state: less helplessness, more self-respect.

Body / energy: less collapse after decisions.

---

## 5. What Stabilized

Stable gains:
- the person can notice compression earlier;
- action is possible when the step is small and clear;
- self-support is less dependent on external approval.

New behavior: acting before complete inner certainty.

New support: rhythm, tracking, and one safe visible action per week.

---

## 6. What Still Blocks

Remaining bottleneck: Fire is still cautious. Visibility may create tension.

Hidden mechanism still active: the system still connects expression with possible rejection.

Risk of regression: trying to jump too fast into large visibility.

---

## 7. Updated Wu Xing Map

Water: improved from low to moderate-low. Safety is stronger, but not fully stable.

Wood: more active. Direction is clearer.

Fire: still sensitive. Expression is possible but requires safety.

Earth: more stable. Routine helps.

Metal: clearer value and boundaries.

Main change: Water now supports Wood better.

Weakest element now: Fire.

First element to support next: Fire, while keeping Water stable.

## 8. Main Mechanism Change

Before: fear created compression and avoidance.

Now: fear appears, but action can still happen.

What this means: the person is moving from pure protection toward first manifestation.

Resource focus: support Fire without overheating the system.

Remedies / tools: needs verification based on current response.

What to track:
