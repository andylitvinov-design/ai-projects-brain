# Sample Remedy / Support Note

This is a fictional, anonymized example. It contains no private client data.

---

## 1. State

Short state summary: the person feels compressed before action and loses energy when visibility is required.

Main emotion: fear mixed with shame.

Main conflict: desire to move outward vs need to stay protected.

Main protection: hesitation, withdrawal, and postponement.

---

## 2. Support Direction

What needs support: basic safety and permission to act without full certainty.

Why now: the person is not in full collapse, but the first manifestation is still fragile.

Which resource / element it supports: Water first, then Fire and Wood.

---

## 3. Tool / Remedy Logic

Tool / remedy: needs verification.

Role: soften compression and support first movement.

Why it fits: the situation is not only lack of motivation; it is a protective response to visibility.

What it may support: calmer contact, less hesitation, better tolerance of action.

Needs verification: remedy choice, potency, sensitivity, and previous response.

---

## 4. What To Track

Emotional response: less shame, less fear before action.

Body / energy response: less tension, less exhaustion after small visible steps.

Behavioral change: faster completion of simple actions.

Timing: track for several days and compare before/after.

---

## 5. When To Reassess

Reassess after a short observation period or after the first clear response.

## 6. Safety Note
