# Sample Full Client Report

This is a fictional, anonymized example. It contains no private client data.

---

## 1. Request

Client request: low energy, fear of action, difficulty moving toward a professional goal.

Context: the client wants more visibility and income, but feels inner compression when action is needed.

Direct client language: “I know what I want, but something stops me.”

---

## 2. Current State

The person has a forming inner structure, but the system is still protective. There is enough strength to understand the goal, but not enough stable resource to move outward freely.

Main signals:

- energy: unstable;
- clarity: moderate;
- confidence: fragile;
- action: delayed;
- work / money: desire exists, but movement is blocked.

---

## 3. Self + Goal Field

Current Self field: cautious, compressed, trying not to lose support.

Goal field: more visible, active, financially stronger.

Contradiction: the goal requires expansion, but the current Self still protects stability.

Missing resource: safe permission to manifest without losing inner support.

---

## 4. Dao Level And Stage

Current level: 3.0–3.5.

Stage / image: Fortress.

What this level means: the inner fortress is forming. The person is not in collapse, but still needs protection and structure.

Main task: strengthen the Self and create safe first manifestation.

Risk: pushing too hard may increase defense and avoidance.

Next realistic level: 3.5–4.0, King / Queen.

Final state: “I can occupy my place.”

One-cycle goal: move from protected stability to safe expression.

---

## 5. Hidden Mechanism

Impulse: desire to grow, earn more, become visible.

Compression: fear of exposure and possible rejection.

Defense: hesitation, postponement, overthinking.

Repetition: the person wants movement, but delays the next step.

Exhaustion: energy drains into inner tension instead of action.

Simple explanation: the problem is not lack of desire. The system wants movement but still treats visibility as unsafe.

The deeper mechanism is fear of visibility and loss of inner safety.

The first support should be Water: basic safety, trust, rest, and softening compression.

The next movement is one safe visible action, not a large breakthrough.
