# Sample Express Diagnosis

This is a fictional, anonymized example. It contains no private client data.

---

## Request

Confidence and low energy.

## Current State

The person has some inner support, but feels uncertainty about the next step. There is emotional compression, hesitation, and low drive for action.

## Dao / Resource Level

Current level: 3.0–3.5 / Fortress stage.

Meaning:
The inner fortress is forming. The person has some support, but is not yet confident enough to move outward freely.

## Wu Xing Resource Map

Water: low — anxiety, hesitation, weak deep support.
Wood: moderate — desire to grow exists, but movement is blocked.
Fire: moderate-low — expression is possible, but shy.
Earth: moderate — some support exists.
Metal: moderate — boundaries exist, but need more clarity and permission.

## Main Bottleneck

Water is the first bottleneck. Without stronger Water, Wood cannot grow confidently.

## Hidden Mechanism

Impulse: desire to act and become more visible.
Compression: fear of a larger step.
Defense: hesitation and avoidance.
Repetition: wanting movement but delaying action.
Exhaustion: loss of strength and confidence.

## Support Direction

First restore Water: inner support, relaxation, trust, and basic confidence.

Then support Wood: direction, courage, and first action.

## Practical Next Step

1. Reduce pressure.
2. Restore deep support.
3. Choose one safe action toward the goal.
4. Recheck state after 1–2 weeks.
