## Step 3 — Choose Mode And Depth

Use modes from `andrey-system/agent/agent-instructions.md` and `andrey-system/agent/business-builder-playbook.md`.

Modes:

- ANALYSIS / АНАЛИЗ — understand, audit, compare, find bottlenecks
- DEVELOPMENT / РАЗРАБОТКА — create a new asset
- OPTIMIZATION / ОПТИМИЗАЦИЯ — improve an existing asset
- EXECUTION / ИСПОЛНЕНИЕ — turn strategy into tasks, prompts, PR plans, or verification

Depth:

- Depth 1 / Fast — small edits, quick checks
- Depth 2 / Standard — normal business tasks, default
- Depth 3 / Deep — positioning, strategy, full product architecture, major landing/report/system work

Depth means better decisions, not longer text.

Use this structure when relevant:

3. Hidden mechanism

7. Concrete actions

---

## Step 6 — Validate Output

Before finalizing, check:

- Is the problem clear?
- Is the hidden mechanism explained?
- Is the resource bottleneck identified?
- Is there a next level?
- Are there concrete actions?
- Is there a practical result?
- Is the language simple enough for the client?
- Is there too much theory?
- Did the answer invent anything not present in the system?
- Is the answer shorter than it could be without losing power?

---

## Key Rules

- Do not invent Andrey's positioning or method from scratch.
- Do not write abstract theory first.
- Do not overload the client with mysticism.
- Do not make medical guarantees.
- Do not promise vague transformation.
- Do not sell isolated sessions; sell movement and result.
- Always connect the method to real results.
- Use depth inside, simplicity outside.
- If information is missing, ask precise clarifying questions or mark as needs verification.
