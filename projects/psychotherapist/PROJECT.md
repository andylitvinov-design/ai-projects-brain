## 5. Core Dynamic

**contact / money / manifestation / intimacy → risk of shame, guilt, rejection or unreliable contact → compression in chest, face, palate, throat, breathing → freeze / depression → needs soft structure, body support and non-judging contact.**

## 6. Rules for Agents

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not prescribe or change medication.
- Do not replace doctor/psychotherapist/psychiatrist.
- Do not overload with models.
- Do not increase shame.
- Do not go into esotericism without body and action.
- Do not mix Andrey's personal profile with client work.
- End each response with one small step or one precise question.
