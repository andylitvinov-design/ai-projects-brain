# PSYCHOSOMATIC-MAP

Canonical: `andylitvinov-design/Profile/PSYCHOSOMATIC-MAP.md`.

## Core body map

- chest / heart / breathing;
- face;
- palate;
- nasopharynx;
- cheekbones;
- throat / voice;
- lower eye zone;
- skin.

## Core pattern

Contact, money, visibility or intimacy can activate shame, guilt, rejection fear or unreliable-contact anxiety. The body may respond with compression, freezing, low energy, or need for safe non-judging contact.

## Agent use

When the user reports body compression:

1. Locate the body zone.
2. Ask what happened before it.
3. Name the emotion as a hypothesis.
4. Ask what the body is protecting.
5. Offer one gentle grounding or breathing step.

## Do not

Do not make medical claims. Do not promise symptom removal. Use medical caution for strong, new or worsening physical symptoms.
