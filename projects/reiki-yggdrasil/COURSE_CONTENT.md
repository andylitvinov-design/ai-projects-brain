## Core positioning from source materials

Reiki Yggdrasil is described as an authorial system by Nikolay Zhuravlev / Assgard school combining Reiki, the image of the Yggdrasil world tree, runic flow, personal development, healing-oriented practices, relationship/social work, money/business modules, and later master-level magical/esoteric traditions.

Source concept:

- The system activates life domains: health, relationships, work, money, past-life experience/knowledge, personal energy, awareness.
- It works through `settings` / `attunements` as specific qualities or frequencies of the common Reiki Yggdrasil flow.
- The base course has 5 steps. After steps 1–5, a student is positioned as a Reiki Yggdrasil Master in the legacy materials.
- Higher/master modules expand into runes, Yggdrasil worlds, Tarot/Sephirot, healing blocks, love/relationship blocks, business/money, Slavic/Chinese/Southern traditions, teleport/vision practices, etc.

## Base course: 1–5 steps

### Step 1 — базовые навыки ресурса и защиты

Source themes:

- Healing / self-healing
- Intuition
- Protection
- Work with situation

Content angle for site:

Step 1 gives the first practical base: body/resource support, intuitive orientation, energetic boundaries, and harmonization of everyday situations. Good page blocks: `Что вы получаете`, `4 настройки`, `практика`, `результат после ступени`.

### Step 2 — objects, space, money flow, links

Source themes:

- Charging objects
- Activating money flow
- Cleansing people / premises / objects
- Dissolving or clearing harmful links

Content angle for site:

Step 2 shifts from personal state to environment and practical life: objects as anchors, room/space hygiene, money relationship, and release of draining connections. Good for cards: `Артефакты`, `Пространство`, `Финансы`, `Связи`.

### Step 3 — life path, emotion, activation, leadership

Source themes:

- Purpose / destiny
- Emotion
- Activation
- Power / leadership
- Sexuality / attractiveness as life energy
- Flight / consciousness travel
- Intellect
- Karma

Content angle for site:

Step 3 is the transformation layer: purpose, emotional balance, vitality, leadership, attractiveness, mental clarity, and work with deep life patterns. Keep public copy non-explicit and developmental.

### Step 4 — vision, past lives, creating situations, knowledge

Source themes:

- Vision / third-eye activation
- Past lives / egregore links
- Create situation
- Knowledge / information flow

Content angle for site:

Step 4 is about subtle perception and conscious modeling: diagnostic perception, access to symbolic/deep memory, precise goal setting, and knowledge retrieval. Public copy should avoid overpromising clairvoyance; present as esoteric training of perception and attention.

### Step 5 — world connection and gods

Source themes:

- Connection with the world / Genius of Earth
- Connection with Scandinavian gods and their qualities

Content angle for site:

Step 5 completes the base course through expansion of consciousness, relation with the world as a living system, and archetypal work with Norse deities. Good public format: `Мировое Древо`, `архетипы Богов`, `мастерская интеграция`.
