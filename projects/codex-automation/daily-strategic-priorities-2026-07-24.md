## Candidate score rubric

`priority = impact × urgency × completion_probability ÷ risk`

1–5 meanings used here:

- Impact: `5` portfolio-wide reliability or critical security; `3` material single-project product/business evidence.
- Urgency: `5` unresolved production regression or high-severity security boundary; `3` strategically stalled product without current evidence.
- Completion probability: `4` bounded audit/evidence work is independently executable; `3` contained implementation is feasible but needs full regression proof; `2` one exact owner action is missing.
- Risk: `1` read-only verification or existing canonical recovery path; `3` authorization change with external-side-effect risk.
