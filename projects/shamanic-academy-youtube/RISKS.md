# Risks - shamanic-academy-youtube

## Data Risks

- YouTube public tabs can change order, visibility, and metadata availability.
- RSS is incomplete and should not be used as the only full archive source.
- Some entries are flat-list-only and may be unavailable, private, deleted, or region-limited.

## Classification Risks

- Rule-based labels may not match the creator's intended course taxonomy.
- `high` priority means reusable potential, not approved publication status.
- Shorts may still contain practice value, but are categorized as `short` by source tab.

## Safety Risks

- Do not add cookies, OAuth tokens, Studio data, private analytics, or account exports.
- Do not treat public video descriptions as consent to republish full transcripts or private course material.

## Mitigations

- Keep uncertainty in `needs_verification`.
- Review high-priority candidates manually before product/course reuse.
- Re-run public collection commands when refreshing the index.
