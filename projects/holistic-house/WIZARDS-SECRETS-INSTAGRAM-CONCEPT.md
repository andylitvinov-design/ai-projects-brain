# Wizards Secrets — Instagram Brand & Content Concept
**Status:** Approved concept direction  
**Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Instagram handle:** `@wizards.secrets` (preferred; availability and current ownership must be checked in Instagram)  
**Brand relationship:** An entertainment-led discovery channel by Holistic House.

## 1. Brand idea

**Wizards Secrets** is a playful, magical world where memorable wizard characters share funny observations, short aphorisms, curious human truths and small discoveries about everyday life.

The audience comes for entertainment, wit and the fantasy atmosphere; stays because the content feels relatable and shareable; and can discover practical self-knowledge tools through Holistic House.

The account is not a therapy page, an occult teaching account, a formal academy or a Harry Potter fan account.

## 2. Audience promise

A visitor should understand immediately:
- This account offers short, entertaining wizard-style secrets about life, love, emotions, habits and human nature.
- The content is witty, curious and easy to share — never preachy.
- There is a path from entertaining discoveries to free self-discovery tests and useful resources at Holistic House.

**Core promise:** A little magic. A lot of insight about life.

Suggested supporting line: **Funny secrets. Curious minds. A little everyday magic.**

## 4. Audience and language

- Primary launch language: English, designed for broad European and international comprehension.
- Use simple, natural, conversational English; avoid idioms that are difficult for non-native speakers.
- Keep on-screen text concise and add English captions/subtitles.
- Test localized versions (e.g. Spanish) only after the English content format demonstrates traction.
- Broad adult audience, with creative and topic choices suitable for approximately 20–55; do not make the brand feel like children's entertainment.

## 5. Content pillars

1. **Wizards on everyday life (30%)** — witty observations about work, habits, procrastination, confidence, stress and daily absurdities.
2. **Love & relationships (25%)** — humorous truths about attraction, communication, boundaries, expectations and misunderstandings; no moralising or simplistic diagnosis.
3. **Human nature & self-discovery (20%)** — personality, emotional patterns, strengths, blind spots and curiosity about why people behave as they do.
4. **Tiny life secrets (15%)** — memorable aphorisms, reframes, surprising perspectives and practical micro-insights.
5. **Holistic House discoveries (10%)** — free tests, self-assessment tools and useful next steps, presented as invitations rather than hard sales.

These are starting editorial proportions, not fixed quotas; adjust using measured watch time, shares, saves, profile visits, follows and link clicks.

## 6. Character universe

Build original characters with distinct voices and visual identities. The world may evoke classic fantasy and magical-school wonder, but must remain original and must not imply affiliation with Harry Potter, Hogwarts, Warner Bros. or other third-party franchises.

- **The Wise Wizard:** dry wit, calm confidence, concise observations about human nature. Main recurring face and anchor character.
- **The Young Apprentice:** curious, impulsive, asks the questions the audience might ask; creates comic contrast with the Wise Wizard.
- **The Enchantress:** playful insight into love, emotions, intuition and relationships; never reduced to stereotypes.
- **The Alchemist:** experiments with habits, routines, perspective and everyday behaviour; frames ideas as playful experiments, not medical claims.

Start with two characters (Wise Wizard + Young Apprentice) to build recognition and consistency. Introduce the others after the first format is established.

## 7. Tone and editorial rules

- Entertain first; leave the viewer with a thought, not a lecture.
- Short, quotable, witty, slightly mischievous and warm.
- No preachiness, scolding, guru posturing or grand promises.
- Prefer one memorable idea per video.
- Use relatable situations, reversals, irony, clever dialogue and gentle self-recognition.
- Avoid claims that a short video can diagnose, treat or solve mental-health conditions.
- Do not present homeopathy, energy work or spiritual ideas as scientifically established medical treatment.
- Make humour inclusive and never humiliate vulnerable groups or individuals.
- Keep the magical styling as a storytelling device; do not imply supernatural powers or guaranteed outcomes.

## 8. Repeatable short-video formats

Default production format: **30 seconds**, typically six scenes of about five seconds each. Each concept ends with a scene-by-scene storyboard.

Suggested recurring series:
- **A Wizard’s Secret:** one unexpected truth about life.
- **Apprentice vs Wizard:** a short comic question-and-answer.
- **Love According to Wizards:** funny, recognisable relationship observations.
- **Human Nature Is Weird:** surprising everyday behaviour.
- **Spell of the Day:** a playful metaphor for a useful habit or perspective — not a literal supernatural promise.
- **Which Wizard Are You?:** character-led prompts linked to a relevant free test.
- **The Wizard’s Reply:** respond to audience questions in the wizard’s voice.

Typical 30-second structure:
1. 0–3 sec — visual or verbal hook.
2. 3–8 sec — relatable situation or question.
3. 8–18 sec — wizard/apprentice exchange or unexpected turn.
4. 18–25 sec — punchline or memorable insight.
5. 25–30 sec — light CTA: share, follow, comment or explore a free test.

Do not force a sales CTA into every video. Most videos should earn follows and shares; selected videos should lead to free tests.

## 10. Growth and conversion path

**Discovery:** Reels with strong first-second hooks, recognisable characters, humour and universally relatable situations.  
**Engagement:** invite comments with simple questions; use polls and story prompts; answer selected questions in character.  
**Follow:** build repeatable series so visitors know what they will get by subscribing.  
**Profile visit:** consistent avatar, display name and bio make the promise immediately clear.  
**Holistic House conversion:** selected Reels and Stories invite users to take a free test or explore a self-discovery resource.  
**Measurement:** track reach, 3-second hold, average watch time, completion, shares, saves, profile visits, follows per reach, link clicks and test starts/completions. Use UTM links to distinguish Instagram traffic.

## 11. Visual direction

- Original wizard characters with strong silhouettes and expressive faces.
- A cinematic, warm, mysterious fantasy atmosphere, balanced with humour and approachable charm.
- A consistent palette and typography; readable text on mobile.
- Give each recurring character a distinct colour accent or visual signature.
- Avoid direct copying of recognisable Harry Potter costumes, emblems, house uniforms, props, character likenesses, spell names, logos or franchise typography.
- Avoid generic stock fantasy imagery that changes character appearance from Reel to Reel; maintain character reference images and visual continuity.

## 12. Launch sequence

1. Confirm that `@wizards.secrets` is available and can be secured; do not claim it is available until checked directly in Instagram.
2. Create the main Wise Wizard avatar and Young Apprentice character sheet.
3. Finalise profile image, display name, bio, link-in-bio and a focused free-tests landing page.
4. Produce 9 launch Reels: 3 everyday-life humour, 2 relationship observations, 2 human-nature secrets, 1 character introduction and 1 free-test invitation.
5. Publish consistently and review results after the first 2–3 weeks; retain formats that drive shares, saves, follows and test starts.
6. Expand character universe and language variants only after a recognisable winning format emerges.

- **Relationship:** “Wizards Secrets is an entertainment and discovery project by Holistic House.”

## 14. Naming and IP guardrails

“Wizards Secrets” is a working brand direction approved for concept development, not a verified trademark or cleared legal name. Check Instagram handle availability, domain conflicts and relevant trademark registers before investing in packaging or paid promotion. Build original character names, backstories, costumes, dialogue and visual assets; use fantasy as a broad genre reference rather than reproducing protected franchise elements.
