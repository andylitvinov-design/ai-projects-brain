## Template rules

- Keep entries short: usually 5-15 bullets total and under about 40 lines.
- Prefer bullets over narrative.
- Do not duplicate the final `/delivery` report.
- Record only reusable facts and lessons.
- If unknown, write `needs verification`.
- Use exact file names and exact commands when available.
- Do not store secrets, env values, tokens, cookies, private credentials, raw
  chat history, or broad logs.
- Default new lessons to `proposal` or `needs verification` until validated.
