# Проверь дизайн
Use actual browser comparison, not just a visual opinion. Follow full canonical workflow at https://github.com/andylitvinov-design/ai-projects-brain/blob/main/agent-skills/check-design.md . Find the approved reference, capture real site at matching viewport/state, compare geometry/text/photos/buttons, make minimal corrections, and recapture until verified parity. Then call Finish to LIVE and compare a fresh production screenshot. Preserve the reference and task state between real worker cycles. Do not claim exactness without screenshots or future execution without an authorized running worker.
